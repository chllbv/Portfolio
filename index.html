Case Study

Proof of work.

Case Study · 01
How I delivered conflict-free interview scheduling for a full recruitment cycle.
ClientExecutive Board
Duration1 week

Problem

With 8 board members and 15+ applicants needing to align schedules for interviews, coordinating everyone's availability manually risked double-bookings and conflicts within a tight one-week window.

Solution

I managed the full interview scheduling process, collecting availability from all 8 board members, setting up a Calendly system connected to Google Calendar, and giving applicants a 3-day window to confirm their slot.

Result

Zero scheduling conflicts across 15+ applicant interviews. All interviews completed within the one-week window, with no manual back-and-forth needed.

"Everything went smoothly, no scheduling conflicts at all. Thank you for handling this so well." — Virla, Director of Media
